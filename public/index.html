Working rule of thumb: copy and paste from the manuscript, capture only the strongest lines, and keep moving past maybes.

  • Stay under roughly 3 excerpts per poem and 30 per book.
  • Aim for plenty of excerpts in the 10–25 word range.
  • Use Cmd/Ctrl + Enter to submit quickly from a text box.
