# Reviewing videos in Weaver

Open [Weaver](https://weaver.buttonpoetry.com/) or the task link from Footage Helper. Enter your work email and click **Load videos**.

Choose what you want to see under **Show**:

- **My review queue:** videos that still need your review. Save your rating, with excerpts when appropriate, to move to the next video.
- **My reviewed videos:** your saved reviews. Open a video here to revisit it, update your rating or add an excerpt.
- **Video library:** every video, including reviewed and sent videos. Choose an event or All events, search for an author/title, or filter by status.

The number beside each event says **awaiting your review**, **reviewed by you**, or **videos total**, depending on the selected view. Personal counts use the email entered above.

Excerpts are optional when saving a Weaver video rating. If a save times out, keep the page open and read the nearby status before retrying; the tool checks the saved record without repeating the write.

**Review complete** means the video has met the current review minimum. **Ready for handoff** means it can move toward Poetry Please. **Needs editing** and **Needs a check** show remaining work. **Sent to Poetry Please** means a handoff is recorded; publishing and calendar placement happen later.

If a video has enough reviewers but still needs an excerpt, add that excerpt through your saved review. Updating your own review does not add another independent vote.

Keep the A/B or other version label in mind when choosing between versions of the same poem. Each video keeps its own review history.

## Resolving files and releasing videos

Both original media (OM) and finished video (FV) enter the same review process. A file being reviewed does not mean it is ready to publish.

**Save file links** records the exact files, formats, selected primary and pairing evidence. It does not change the release decision or select excerpts. **Save release decision** controls Ready, Hold and Reject. Linking a file never clears a hold. The standing policy can subsequently advance a verified recording at an average of 8.5 with five independent reviewers and at least one collected excerpt. Excerpt counts remain visible and add no score bonus.

The scheduled resolver follows these rules:

1. Verify whether the reviewed file is FV or OM. Unknown or conflicting roles appear in the resolution queue.
2. For FV, verify the exact reviewed-file lineage and current media version before using it.
3. For OM, search the event's confirmed finished-edit folders, including nested folders. Match the event, performer and title exactly, with one reviewed recording. Retain distinct resolutions and formats as separate assets. Prefer the unique highest-resolution vertical output, otherwise the unique highest-resolution output; ties require a choice.
4. Create one durable editing request only after a complete, successful search finds no match. An unavailable folder, ambiguous recording, changed file or missing search scope creates a resolution task instead. A previously linked FV must be resolved before requesting a replacement edit.
5. Recheck outstanding editing requests on later scheduled runs. An eligible completed output resolves the request and passes through the separate release-policy check. Explicit rework instructions require confirmation of the completed rework.
6. Check the selected file's exact ID, size, checksum and video metadata at delivery. Existing delivery claims and receiver records prevent blind resends or replacement of an accepted primary.

Administrators can maintain each event's finished-edit search scope and resolve exceptions in **Media resolution & editing queue**. When no finished-edit folder exists, explicitly confirm that fact as the event scope. Editors upload completed files to the confirmed folders or link them in Weaver. The queue is durable; it does not require a browser to stay open.

Poetry Please owns receiver media verification, caption approval and registration of additional renditions for an already received video. Calendar readiness requires fresh receiver evidence tying the selected media and approved caption to the canonical video. Weaver review completion or a collected excerpt alone does not establish that readiness.
